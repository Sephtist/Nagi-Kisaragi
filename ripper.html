THE RIPPER CASE

CASE FILE // ARCHIVED

CASE STATUS: RESOLVED

INVESTIGATOR: NAGI KISARAGI

CLASSIFICATION: CLOSED

CASE OVERVIEW

A series of ten murders committed over a period of approximately six months became one of the most notorious unsolved investigations of its time.

The victims were discovered physically connected to one another through extensive stitching, creating a disturbing and deliberate pattern that became the defining signature of the case.

Despite extensive investigation, authorities were unable to identify a viable suspect or establish sufficient evidence to resolve the murders.

ORIGINAL INVESTIGATION

The investigation eventually became inactive due to the absence of a reliable suspect and the deterioration of available evidence over time.

The case remained publicly notorious despite its lack of resolution.

KISARAGI'S INVOLVEMENT

Kisaragi became interested in the case primarily because of its notoriety. Rather than accepting the established conclusion that the investigation had simply reached a dead end, he began reconstructing the case independently.

His investigation focused less on identifying a physical suspect and more on understanding the reasoning behind the murders.

[NAGI'S NOTE: Everyone kept asking what kind of person would do this. That wasn't the useful question.]

CASE STATUS

RESOLVED.

The perpetrator was identified while still alive and was subsequently brought into custody.

Full investigative documentation is retained within the restricted archive.
